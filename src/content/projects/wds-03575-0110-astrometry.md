## Scientific question

How have the separation and position angle of WDS 03575-0110 changed over time, and what orbital parameters can be refined from combining new and historical measurements?

## Data and materials

- Robotic telescope observations from Las Cumbres Observatory
- Historical astrometric measurements of WDS 03575-0110
- Gaia DR3 astrometric data for comparison

## Methods

- Measurement of separation and position angle using AstroImageJ
- Comparison with historical measurements and Gaia DR3
- Common proper-motion evaluation
- Orbital parameter estimation using Desmos fitting

## My contribution

- Conducted astrometric analysis using Las Cumbres Observatory data
- Used AstroImageJ to measure separation and position angle
